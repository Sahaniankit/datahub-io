RESERVED

Vande Bharat Express

Assigned seat, booked in advance, built for 300–1,500 km intercity runs.

  • ClassesChair Car (CC), Executive (EC)
  • Sleeper variant1 route live — Howrah–Guwahati
  • Booking opens120 days ahead + Tatkal
  • BoardingYour ticketed coach only
UNRESERVED

Namo Bharat Rapid Rail

No seat assignment, walk-up ticket, built for 100–350 km hops — formerly Vande Metro.

  • ClassesSingle AC class, standing allowed
  • Live corridors2 — Ahmedabad–Bhuj, Jayanagar–Patna
  • BookingCounter / UTS app, same day
  • BoardingAny coach, first come first seated

Namo Bharat, stop by stop

Still early — two corridors live today, over a hundred cities planned.

359 km · ~5h 45m · first Namo Bharat corridor, launched 2024

Ahmedabad→ Sabarmati→ Viramgam→ Dhrangadhra→ Halvad→ Samakhiali→ Bhachau→ Gandhidham→ Anjar→ Bhuj

Also live: Jayanagar ↔ Patna (Bihar), run as a 16-coach train for heavier daily demand. Planned next: Chennai–Tirupati, Bhubaneswar–Rourkela, Agra–Mathura, Delhi–Rewari, Lucknow–Kanpur, Secunderabad–Muzaffarpur.

Good to know

”

Vande Bharat trains have carried over 9.1 crore passengers across roughly 1 lakh trips since the first one launched in 2019.

”

The sleeper variant exists on just one route so far — Howrah to Guwahati — making it the only way to do an overnight Vande Bharat journey.

”

Namo Bharat trains are fully air-conditioned despite being unreserved — closer to an AC version of a regular EMU than a budget train.

How this compares to booking apps

This page is for understanding the network at a glance. For live seats, PNR and running status, these do the job well:

IRCTC / RailOne — the official source. Only place that actually issues tickets; best for booking and refunds.

ixigo / Trainman — strong for live running status, seat availability prediction, and platform/coach position.

m-Indicator — best for Mumbai-area suburban + AC local integration alongside long-distance trains.

Route counts, corridors and timings reflect recent public schedules and change as services are added or revised — confirm exact timings, running-day exceptions and fares on IRCTC or NTES before travel.
